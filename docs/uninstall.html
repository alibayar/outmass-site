What made you uninstall?

One tap is enough. No email required, no follow-up.

🔔 Still have an active subscription?

Removing the extension does not cancel your paid subscription. If you were on Starter or Pro:

  • Reinstall OutMass and use Account → Manage Subscription, or
  • Email support@getoutmass.com and we'll cancel + refund the current period.

If it was a mistake…

You can reinstall OutMass from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons — your data, campaigns, and templates are still there waiting for you.

Reinstall OutMass Email support

Removing OutMass completely?

The extension is gone from your browser. If you'd also like to remove the permission you gave OutMass on your Microsoft account, you can do it at account.live.com/consent/Manage (Outlook.com and Hotmail) or myapplications.microsoft.com (work or school accounts). If your IT administrator approved OutMass for your organization, only they can remove that approval. To have your OutMass account and data deleted too, email support@getoutmass.com from the address you signed in with.
