## Do NOT touch (verified traps)

- **r/sales** — zero-tolerance, permanent, non-reversible ban on first
  promo offense. **r/smallbusiness** — all product mentions outside the
  weekly promo thread removed (since 2026-06).
- **Microsoft Tech Community** — zero-tolerance spam policy; usable only
  as slow no-link reputation building.
- **WeChat Official Account** — structurally closed without a Chinese
  entity (no self-serve overseas registration; paid intermediaries only).
- **Zhihu / Juejin / CSDN** — CN-phone gates and/or farming-effort shape.
- **BetaList** ($39–299, pre-launch framing), **YourStory/Inc42** (no
  traction hook yet), **StartupJuncture** (dead), **Chinese CRX mirror
  sites** (unauthorized redistribution risk).
- **Bilibili seeding** (free Pro comps to 2k–50k UP主 for honest reviews)
  is legitimate LATER — but every comp is a discretionary gift: per the
  standing gift rule it needs Ali's explicit per-gift approval, and any
  review must be the creator's own, disclosed, unscripted.

## Sequencing note

Review platforms (G2/TrustRadius/SoftwareSuggest) are profile-now,
reviews-later: profiles can exist empty, and the #61 trigger moments
(bellmed's next big campaign, a thanked support exchange, a converting
prospect) can offer a G2 link with the same breath as a store-review link —
one ask per person, whichever surface they prefer.
