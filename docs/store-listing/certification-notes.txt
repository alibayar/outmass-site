OutMass adds mail merge (personalised bulk email) to Outlook on the web.
Mail is sent from the signed-in user's own mailbox via Microsoft Graph.
Nothing is sent from our servers. No remote code is loaded or executed.

WHERE THE UI APPEARS - please read first
The content script runs ONLY on outlook.live.com, outlook.office.com,
outlook.office365.com and outlook.cloud.microsoft. On any other page the
extension shows nothing, so it cannot be evaluated from a blank tab or the
toolbar alone.

STEPS
1. Install, open https://outlook.live.com, sign in with the account above.
2. Click the OutMass toolbar icon, choose "Sign in with Microsoft", accept
   Microsoft's own consent screen.
3. Back in the Outlook tab, open the side panel from the toolbar popup or
   the round "OM" button at the edge of the page.
4. Upload a CSV: a header of email,firstName and two addresses you control.
5. Write a subject and body, optionally with {{firstName}}. "Send test"
   mails a preview to yourself.
6. "Send" runs the campaign; Reports shows what was delivered.

PLAN-LOCKED FEATURES - intended, not errors
Steps 1-6 are all free. Four controls are plan-limited; selecting one opens
a pricing dialog naming the plans that unlock it. It sends no mail and needs
no payment to see. They are "Auto follow-up for non-openers", "A/B test" and
"Write with AI" (labelled PRO), plus scheduled sending (Starter). Switching
a follow-up on without Pro saves it and says so; the report later offers to
start it.

PERMISSIONS
- Mail.Send: sends the campaign from the signed-in mailbox.
- Mail.Read: requested separately, and only if reply detection is switched
  on. It reads the sender and date of incoming mail so follow-ups stop
  chasing people who replied, and never reads message content.
- storage, alarms, identity; host access to the four domains above and to
  api.getoutmass.com.
